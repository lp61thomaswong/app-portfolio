How I Make Each Video

Six repeatable steps from finding a trader to publishing the review.

1

Pick a trader

I find candidates on Myfxbook, MQL5, or eToro — ones that look popular, viral, or suspiciously good. Controversy drives discovery.

2

Get their stats

Win rate, max drawdown, profit factor, monthly return history, trade duration. Myfxbook and MQL5 let me export full trade history to CSV.

3

Run it through AI

I paste the stats into Claude and ask it to identify red flags — martingale patterns, drawdown spikes, suspicious win streaks, behaviour changes over time.

4

Score them

I apply the 5-category framework: Track Record (20), Risk (30), Returns (20), Behaviour (15), Fit (15). Anything under 60 is an AVOID.

5

Record the video

I screen-record the stats, walk through the AI output live, and give my verdict. I show my reasoning — no mystery, no black box.

6

Post and clip

Full video on YouTube, plus 2–3 Shorts cut from the most surprising moments. The COPY/WATCH/AVOID reveal always makes a great Short.
